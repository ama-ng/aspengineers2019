📌 What to Prepare

Form Requirements

Full Name
Your complete name as it should appear in the album.

Nickname
What your classmates called you. Keep it real!

Phone Number
Active number your classmates can reach you on.

State / Country
Where you're from or currently based.

Passport Photo Link

Current Location

1 Upload your photo to Google Drive → Right-click → "Get link" → Change to "Anyone with the link" → Copy link
2 OR upload to imgur.com → Copy the direct image link (ending in .jpg or .png)
3 Paste that link in the form's "Passport Photo Link" field
